About Me

Here's the deal, I'm Swastik — an undergraduate student who's kinds obsessed with making sense of messy data and teaching machines how to think (or at least pretend they do). I see machine learning as part science, part art, and maybe a little bit of magic sprinkled in.

My philosophy? Keep it curious, keep it real, and never stop experimenting. Whether it's cleaning chaotic datasets or training a model that finally starts to make sense, I live for those little "it worked!" moments that make all the debugging worth it.

I'm the kind of person who'll spend hours tweaking a model's parameters and then celebrate with a random YouTube deep dive on how Netflix recommends movies. I love taking ideas, breaking things just to rebuild them better, and finding beauty in data that others might overlook.

More than anything, I want to build things that actually make a difference — tools, insights, and models that solve real problems.

And if I can do that while learning from awesome people and having a few laughs along the way? Even better.
